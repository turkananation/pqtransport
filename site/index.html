package:pqtransport 0.1.0

Pure-Dart post-quantum transport

Hybrid TLS 1.3 (RFC 10024), encrypted UDP, DNS/DoH/DoT, mDNS, QUIC, and HTTP/1.1–3. Cryptography is exclusively pqforge. Infrastructure is exclusively swissarmyknife. No dart:ffi. No platform TLS on the PQ path.
